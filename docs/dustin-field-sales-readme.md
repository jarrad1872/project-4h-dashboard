## How To Work A Lead

Use one row per real business.

Recommended minimum fields:

- Business or owner label
- City
- Trade domain, usually `pipe.city`
- Missed-call pain signal
- Next action
- Notes from the conversation

Do not mark a business as visited, card-left, demo-booked, trial-started, activated, or paid unless that actually happened.

---

## Stage Meanings

**Prospect:** Looks like a possible fit, but no real contact yet.

**Qualified:** You have a real reason to believe missed calls matter for this business.

**Visited:** You physically stopped by or had a real field touch.

**Card left:** A card was handed to the owner, staff, or left at the business.

**Demo booked:** They agreed to a demo, call, or walkthrough.

**Trial started:** They started the 14-day trial.

**Activated:** Phone is connected or forwarded, one real inbound call was handled, owner summary was received, a lead/job was captured, and the owner has not turned it off.

**Paid:** They converted to the `$39/mo` plan.

**Lost:** Not a fit, declined, or no useful path forward.

## Daily Cadence

Start small and clean.

1. Pick 5-7 nearby plumbing targets.
2. Look for obvious missed-call pain: emergency work, after-hours demand, owner-operated shop, weak phone responsiveness, or reviews mentioning hard-to-reach issues.
3. Visit or call only when it makes sense.
4. Leave a card when there is real curiosity or a real business touch.
5. Update `/sales` the same day.
6. Write the next action clearly enough that Jarrad or Bob can understand what happened later.

Good next actions:

- `Call owner Friday after 3pm`
- `Send demo line after he talks to wife`
- `Check if phone forwarding is possible`
- `Follow up after he tries demo line`
- `Not a fit, already has full-time dispatcher`

Bad next actions:

- `Follow up`
- `Maybe interested`
- `Call later`
